# Radboards brand context

Use this lightweight context for campaign briefs. Review it with the business before production use.

- Radboards sells electric rideables in India: hoverboards, self-balancing scooters, handle hoverboards, electric unicycles, and e-scooters.
- The public website highlights free shipping, Make in India, and lifetime service support.
- Use energetic, confident, practical, safety-conscious Indian English.
- Product claims must be present in current scraped product data or separately approved. Never invent discounts, delivery promises, warranties, speed, range, suitability, or safety claims.
- Default goal: create interest and invite a viewer to explore the specific product page.
